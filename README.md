## 🏛️ 01 — Gebze Dijital
> **Municipal Citizen Services Mobile Platform**  
> `Status:` 🚧 *In Active Development* &nbsp;|&nbsp; `Category:` *Mobile & Cloud (Institutional)*

A cross-platform mobile application designed to bridge the communication gap between local government and citizens, digitizing city services into an accessible mobile interface.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Announcements & Press:** Real-time municipal news, bulletins, and event calendars.
- **Utility Outage Alerts:** Live notifications and schedules for planned water and electricity interruptions.
- **On-Duty Pharmacies (Nöbetçi Eczaneler):** Location-based directory with instant navigation routing.
- **Smart Parking & City Guide:** City landmarks, recreational areas, and municipal parking status.
- **Civic Requests & Feedback:** Citizen issue ticketing system with progress tracking.
- **Gebze Pati:** Animal adoption, stray care services, and veterinary service updates.
- **Emergency Hub:** Quick-access emergency contacts, shelter points, and disaster instructions.

## 🧾 03 — POS System
> **Real-Time Restaurant POS Ecosystem**  
> `Status:` 🚧 *In Active Development* &nbsp;|&nbsp; `Category:` *Full-Stack, Real-Time & Web/Mobile*

A comprehensive multi-role restaurant ecosystem integrating kitchen displays, waiter mobile terminals, and management dashboards through real-time bidirectional communication.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Tailored views for Managers, Waitstaff, Cashiers, and Kitchen crews.
- **Real-Time Kitchen Display System (KDS):** Instant ticket dispatch via WebSockets with audio-visual alerts.
- **JWT & Secure Authentication:** Encrypted token authentication with granular route permissions.
- **Transactional Consistency:** Relational integrity for split checks, partial payments, and item voids.

## 🏢 04 — Gebze Municipality Asset Management
> **Enterprise Asset, Facility & Inventory Tracking Platform**  
> `Status:` 🏛️ *In Development (Institutional Enterprise)* &nbsp;|&nbsp; `Category:` *Enterprise Systems*

An enterprise platform developed to catalog, track, and audit physical, technological, and infrastructural assets across municipal directorates and administrative facilities.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Hierarchical Facility Modeling:** Multi-tier mapping of administrative buildings, floors, departments, and rooms.
- **Asset Lifecycle Management:** From acquisition and barcode tagging to maintenance schedules and depreciation.
- **Custody & Transfer Workflows:** Secure handoff records between personnel with formal sign-off tracking.
- **Audit Trails & Movement Logs:** Tamper-evident activity logs detailing every location and assignment change.
- **Maintenance & Diagnostics:** Incident ticketing, external repair vendor logs, and return validations.
